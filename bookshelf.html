My Bookshelf

The reading list I actually hand to product managers who ask.

Product work sits between three things that rarely appear in the same book: how systems are built, how organisations behave, and how people decide under uncertainty. This shelf is my attempt to keep all three sharp. It is grouped the way I think about the job — strategy first, then the org, then the numbers, then the machine underneath. Nothing here is on the list because it is famous; each one changed how I do something.
