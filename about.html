Who I Am

I came into product through data science, but I learned that the job is less about owning a roadmap and more about creating shared conviction. I start by understanding who makes the decisions, what success means to each stakeholder, and where customer needs overlap with business and technical constraints. That gives the team a problem worth solving and a clear way to judge whether we are making progress.

From there, I work in short, evidence-led loops: study how people solve the problem today, prototype competing ideas, define the value proposition and north-star metric, and cut the MVP to the smallest useful end-to-end experience. Engineering, design and go-to-market stay involved throughout. The goal is not to ship more features; it is to remove assumptions until a useful product can earn adoption and scale with confidence.

Align Before Building

I map the decision-makers, their incentives and their measures of success before shaping the solution. A small product council brings engineering, sales, marketing, platform and customer voices into the same room, while one-to-one conversations surface the concerns people may not raise in a group. Alignment starts with making the trade-offs visible.

Turn Debate Into Evidence

When strong opinions compete, I prototype the alternatives and learn from the friction. Customer research, current workarounds, technical constraints and early usability tests turn abstract preferences into a shared problem statement. I use dissent to improve the decision, then commit the group to one value proposition and one measurable north star.

Ship, Learn, Then Scale

I reduce the idea to a useful end-to-end slice, define what is deliberately out of scope, and build in short cycles with frequent demos. Clear ownership and psychological safety let the team move quickly without hiding risk. Feedback shapes the next iteration, and usage—not internal enthusiasm—decides what becomes part of the platform.

What I Do Best

AI Platform Strategy

Shaping the product, architecture and operating model together — knowing when to add an AI layer to an established platform, when to build AI-native, and how to turn the first valuable use case into a reusable platform that can earn adoption at scale.

Agentic Systems & LLMOps

Turning capable models into dependable enterprise agents by designing the harness around them: memory, skills, tools, permissions and connectors, with evaluation, observability and release discipline built into the product rather than added after the demo.

Evidence-Led Product Discovery

Turning competing stakeholder visions into testable hypotheses through customer research, rapid prototypes and current-workflow analysis. I define one measurable north star, cut the MVP to the smallest useful journey, and let usability and adoption guide the next bet.

Teams & Go-To-Market

Leading cross-functional agile teams of data scientists, engineers and designers, and owning the pitch, pricing, positioning and patents that turn the build into revenue.
