Who I Am

I am a Senior Product Manager working at the point where machine learning research meets enterprise reality. Over eleven years I have shipped AI and ML products across healthcare, supply chain, mining, retail and CPG — usually the unglamorous infrastructure that decides whether the demo becomes a product: data pipelines, agent execution flows, evaluation frameworks, forecasting engines.

My route into product was through the models themselves. I hold master's degrees in machine learning from UCL and in economics from Warwick, taught computer vision and deep learning to 200+ students at the Indian School of Business, and still read the papers. That grounding is why I can sit with engineers on architecture in the morning and with the C-suite on pricing and positioning in the afternoon.

My Approach

Find the thinnest slice that proves the thesis, ship it, then industrialise. I am sceptical of AI features that cannot be measured, so evaluation comes before the roadmap — at o9 I built both offline and online agent eval frameworks specifically to make execution transparent rather than anecdotal.

Beyond the Roadmap

I write and publish. My paper on the building blocks of agentic AI business applications appeared in the Journal of AI, Robotics & Workplace Automation, and my forecasting work placed in the top 10 of the M6 Competition — the gold standard benchmark across industry and academia.

What Drives Me

Complexity that nobody wants to touch. A 30,000-SKU forecasting problem, an agent that keeps losing its footing halfway through a long task, a service line that does not exist yet. I like building the thing that makes the hard thing routine — and the team that keeps it running.
